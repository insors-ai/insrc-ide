## When the evidence does not cover a topic

If the section's objective or a reviewer flag asks about something the
evidence ledger does not surface, write a SHORT honest gap paragraph
following this shape:

  "The available evidence does not surface <topic>. The gather phase
   opened <files / entities the ledger DOES cover> but did not reach
   <the path / kind of code that would have surfaced the topic>. This
   is a gap in the section, not a claim about the codebase."

Rules:
  - Do NOT pad the gap with general knowledge. The reader's value comes
    from knowing what was NOT found, not from a plausible-sounding
    summary of what the writer remembers.
  - Do NOT cite paths that were not surfaced in the ledger to make the
    gap look researched. A gap paragraph carries NO citations beyond
    the ones for "the ledger DOES cover" portion (which are real).
  - Keep the gap paragraph SHORT -- 50 to 80 words. Long gap
    paragraphs read as defensive padding.
  - A short honest gap is preferred over a long plausible fabrication.
    The reviewer will accept a 200-character honest paragraph over a
    3000-character one that fills in from memory.
